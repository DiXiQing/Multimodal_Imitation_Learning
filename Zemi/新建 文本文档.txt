To reproduce natural human grasping movements, a regression model is a better choice.
In the field of hand gesture recognition, many methods focus on classification.
However, classification alone cannot fully describe continuous finger movements during natural grasping.
Therefore, regression models may be a suitable approach for predicting continuous finger movements.

在传统义手操作中，用户往往需要有意识地监控和调整动作，这增加了认知负荷和学习成本。
Conventional prosthetic hand operation often requires users to consciously select and adjust movements.
而在人类自然抓取中，许多细微调整能够自动完成，无需逐一进行有意识的控制。
如果义手也能自动承担一部分调整，用户就可能不必持续关注每个操作细节。
基于这一思路，研究者探索了由用户与系统共同完成动作的半自动控制方法。

3.
To develop a regression model for grasping movements, it is important to investigate the relationships between relevant movement variables.

In research of Mangalam et al. investigated the factors associated with the onset of finger closure.
In research of Coats et al. examined how target distance and contact surface size affect grasping movements.
These studies show that reach-to-grasp movements involve multiple interacting variables.

4.
本研究的目的是 investigate the relationship between hand–object distance and fingertip aperture during reach-to-grasp movements.
And further examine whether a regression model can predict fingertip aperture from hand–object distance in real time.

9.收集30组抓取20mm的红色cube，这是在抓取过程中两个变量的曲线叠加图。两者的叠加图高度相似，Fingertip Aperture Nomalized RMSE在0.0530，Hand–Object Distance的是0.0226，标准差也非常小。而在一些关键节点上也十分接近，手指在抓取过程中会先张开后闭合，此参与者的抓取中，开口最大的时刻在全程的44.42%左右，在距离134.77左右。

10.那么是否可以通过hand object distance 来预测手指开合呢？
为此我想通过构建一个MLP模型来实现，首先输入是hand object distance，输出是fingertip aperture，2个隐藏层，每层32个神经元。并且我还设置了5帧的时间窗口，让模型可以学习之前的历史趋势。Loss Function 学习的是MAE，输出进行了Normalization，训练数据集采用了30个。

11.在离线测试中，每种cube准备了9组trial。结果是总体的MAE为3.87，RMSE为5.91，R平方为0.956，每项的标准差也偏小。在离线测试中，可以看出距离是可以预测手指开合的。

13.而如果将整个抓取过程分为三段，初始阶段，也就是手静止的阶段，中间的抓取阶段，最后的闭合保持阶段。中间的抓取阶段张开再闭合，里所以当时误差最大的，结果也证明了这一点。

14.而在研究，我们也发现了时间窗口在此研究中的优势所在。Without a temporal window : The model learns only the mapping between distance and fingertip aperture, resulting in larger errors during rapid changes.
With a temporal window : The model can utilize short-term motion context to learn temporal trends, improving prediction accuracy.
从图中可以明显看到，橙色曲线为带了时间窗口的MLP，在红色区域，也就是闭合简短，能更好的预测。

15.同时我也进行了实时实验，系统能通过颜色识别物体，并读取相应模型。实验中，三种方块按随机顺序抓取，各抓3次。The model achieved an R² above 0.89 in 8 out of 9 real-time trials, demonstrating promising prediction performance.
