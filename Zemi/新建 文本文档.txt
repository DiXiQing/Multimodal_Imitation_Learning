About 57.7 million people worldwide have traumatic limb amputation. Prosthetic hands aim to restore hand function after upper-limb loss.
Although prosthetic hands have advanced rapidly，But the rejection rate of prosthetic hands is as high as 44%。
One of the main reasons is the high cognitive load when using a prosthetic hand.

3.

In conventional prosthetic hand, users need to consciously select and adjust movements, which increases cognitive load and Learning costs.
In human natural grasping, many fine adjustments occur automatically, without conscious control of each movement.
如果与这种机制结合，Semi-autonomous control may reduce the need for constant user attention.

In Related Work.
Thomas et al 的研究，研究将控制抓取力大小交给系统。系统会先学习用户抓取时的力。结果表明，用户使用半自动控制能提高其的神经效率

Vasile et al 的研究，利用视觉传感器，在抓取过程中将手腕的朝向控制交给了系统，用户只需要控制抓取与放开指令。

These studies show that, Semi-Autonomous control allows the system to handle part of the movement while preserving user control, offering a potential way to reduce the user’s congnitive load.

This is visual information processing，The camera is fixed in the world coordinate frame and provides a top-down view.
The system use MediaPipe and OpenCV to detect hand landmarks and cube。
The visual part will use two variable
Hand–object distance is defined as distance from the fingertip midpoint to the object center. shown by the yellow line.
Fingertip aperture is the distance between the thumb and index fingertips, shown by the blue line.
Both measurements are currently in pixels and will be converted to millimeters in future work.

8.当前模型使用的是一个带窗口的MLP，有两个隐藏层各32个神经元，输入是包含当前时间点和前四个时间点的距离的数组。输出是当前的Fingertip Aperture。Loss Function 采用了 MAE，所有输入会进行Normalization预处理，这次模型的训练使用了30组数据。
In current，the model is used MLP with 5 frame temporal window。and two hidden layers，each 32 neurons。
the input is a vector of distance from current frame and previous four frames。the output is the predicted fingertip aperture at the current frame。
use MAE as the loss function。and normalize the input。this time the model was train using 30 grasping trial。

To evaluate how accurately the model predicts fingertip aperture, we first conducted offline validation using nine trials per cube size, for a total of 27 trials.

Across trials, the mean MAE was 3.85 ± 1.43 pixels, the mean RMSE was 5.52 ± 2.06 pixels, and the mean R-squared was 0.956 ± 0.034.

These results indicate relatively small prediction errors.

如果把整个抓取过程拆开来分为三段，初始和结束阶段是手处于静止的阶段，而抓取阶段是手指开合变动的阶段。
所以在抓取阶段，手的开合预测是更具备挑战的。
所以抓取阶段会是我分析模型预测能力的一个重要指标，但在义手控制中三个阶段都有其的意义。
The grasping process can be divided into three phases: Initial, Movement, and Hold. Fingertip aperture remains stable during the Initial and Hold phases, while it changes during the Movement phase.

Predicting aperture is more challenging, making prediction performance in this phase an important measure of the model’s capability. However, all three phases are relevant to prosthetic hand control.

15.

Without a temporal window : The model learns only the mapping between distance and fingertip aperture, resulting in larger errors during rapid changes.
With a temporal window : The model can utilize short-term motion context to learn temporal trends, improving prediction accuracy.
