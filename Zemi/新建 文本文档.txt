2.In the field of hand gesture recognition
To reproduce natural human grasping movements, a regression model is a better choice.
In the field of hand gesture recognition, many methods focus on classification.
However, classification alone cannot fully describe continuous finger movements during natural grasping.
Therefore, regression models may be a suitable approach for predicting continuous finger movements.

3.To develop a regression model for grasping movements, it is important to investigate the relationships between relevant movement variables.
In the related work have examined these relationships from different perspectives.

Mangalam et al. investigated the factors associated with the onset of finger closure.
Coats et al. examined how target distance and contact surface size affect grasping movements.
These studies suggest that reach-to-grasp movements involve multiple interacting variables.
