Synthetic D3 Window MLP diagnostic report

Primary conclusion
The current generator makes object_area a near-direct phase variable for finger_width.
The 15-frame area history therefore identifies both movement phase and aperture state, leaving little independent information for velocity.
Acceleration is derived from a synthetic velocity trajectory and contains high-frequency noise relative to its useful signal, so it can hurt a finite-capacity MLP.

Recommended next-generation changes
1. Keep area-to-aperture coupling, but add trial-specific aperture offsets or closure timing that are not recoverable from area alone.
2. Vary speed-peak timing independently within a physically plausible range and make the same area history correspond to slightly different aperture trajectories.
3. Generate acceleration from a full 3-D hand velocity trajectory, then add low-amplitude sensor noise separately; avoid making acceleration a noisy copy of a derivative that the target does not use.
4. Add controlled variation in approach speed and closure speed while preserving a single-peaked reach and smooth start/end behavior.
5. Keep the train/test split by complete trial and report per-color results, not only pooled results.
